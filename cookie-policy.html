We keep cookies to a minimum, and nothing optional runs until you say yes.

Last updated: 8 October 2026

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files a website saves in your browser. They let a site remember things between visits, such as a choice you have already made.

Cookies we use

Today we set one cookie: the record of your cookie choice. Analytics and marketing cookies stay off unless you switch them on. We never sell information collected through cookies.

Managing your consent

You can change your choice at any time from here or from "Cookie Settings" in the footer. Withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it, and your browser settings can also block or delete cookies.

Changes to this policy

We update this page when we add or remove a tool, and the date at the top shows the latest change. If a change affects what we ask your consent for, we'll show the cookie banner again.
