Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Overview

Loop Methods ("we," "us," "our") provides enterprise technology consulting, software delivery, and related services. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have — whether you're evaluating our services, submitting a project inquiry, or applying to work with us.

Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as:

  • Contact details submitted through our "Get in Touch" form — name, email, company, and project details
  • Resumes or portfolio material sent to our careers inbox
  • Correspondence you send us by email or phone

We also collect limited technical information automatically, such as browser type and general usage patterns, to keep the site running reliably and secure.

How we use your information

We use the information we collect to respond to inquiries, schedule walkthroughs and calls, evaluate engagements, consider candidates for open roles, and improve our website and services. We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing of information

We do not share your information with third parties except: (a) with service providers who help us operate our business (e.g. email delivery), under confidentiality obligations; (b) if required by law; or (c) with your consent.

Data retention

We retain information for as long as needed to respond to your inquiry, fulfil a contract, or comply with legal obligations, after which it is deleted or anonymized.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.

Contact us
