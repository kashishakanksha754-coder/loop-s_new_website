# Loop Methods — Website Design Brief (for Claude Design)

## Subject & Goal
Loop Methods is an enterprise technology consultancy (20+ years, AI/cloud/mobile delivery, global teams). Design a multi-page marketing website that positions them as a senior, high-trust technology partner — polished enough to sit alongside consultancies like ThoughtWorks or EPAM, with one distinct in-house product (HYRIX AI) spotlighted along the way.

Single job of the site: convince an enterprise buyer this team can be trusted with a serious build, then get them to reach out.

---

## Visual Direction

**Mood:** dark, premium, confident — SaaS-consultancy hybrid, not agency-flashy.

**Palette**
- Background: near-black, #050608 to #0A0B0F
- Card surfaces: frosted glass — white at 3–6% opacity, 1px border at white 8% opacity, ~16–20px radius
- Primary accent: electric blue, #4FA8FF to #7DC4FF
- Secondary accent (HYRIX AI section only): pink-to-purple gradient, isolated to that one section so it reads as a distinct product, not a site-wide theme shift
- Body text: muted gray, ~#9AA0AC
- Headlines: near-white, #F5F6F8

**Type**
- Display face: bold, tight-tracking sans-serif for headlines, weight 600–700, large scale
- Body face: same family or a clean complementary sans, regular weight, generous line-height
- Utility face: small-caps or uppercase tracked-out labels for eyebrows (e.g. "WHAT WE DO", "THE TEAM")

**Layout & signature elements**
- Hero: full-bleed dark background, a large soft abstract 3D orb/blob (blue-to-purple glossy gradient render) bleeding off the bottom-right edge. A hero video is already in production separately — design the layout to host a video background with a dark gradient overlay for text legibility; don't design a static replacement for it.
- Nav: floating pill-shaped bar, dark fill, rounded full corners, active item shown as a filled light pill, solid blue "Get in Touch →" pill button on the far right
- Numbered process steps (01–04): outlined circular badges connected by a thin horizontal line — reserved only for the actual sequential build process, not used decoratively elsewhere
- Project/case study cards: desaturated moody photography, category pill top-left over the image, title + description below, "Know more →" link
- Team cards: black-and-white photography, name + role bottom-left, small LinkedIn glyph top-right; horizontal carousel with dot pagination + arrow nav on Home, full static grid on About
- Buttons: pill-shaped; solid blue fill for primary actions, ghost/outline for secondary
- Subtle dot-grid background texture visible in content sections (not the hero)

**Motion**
- Page-load: gentle fade/rise on hero text
- Scroll-triggered reveals for section headlines and cards (subtle, staggered, not bouncy)
- Hover: slight lift + border glow on cards, arrow nudge on links
- Respect reduced-motion preferences

**Signature element:** the HYRIX AI gradient spotlight — the one deliberate break from the site's disciplined dark/blue system, used exactly once, so it lands as a genuine "product within a company" moment rather than visual noise.

## Consistency Rules
- Numbered 01–04 treatment appears only in "How We Build" — never reused elsewhere as decoration
- Project/service cards share one component design across Home, Services, and Case Studies
- Team carousel = Home preview only; About page uses a full static grid
- HYRIX AI gradient is the single aesthetic departure — keep it contained to that one section/spotlight
- Design mobile breakpoints for: nav (collapse to menu), team carousel (stays swipeable), all grids (stack to 1 column)
